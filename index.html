KNOW YOUR MOVES

FIGHT SMART.

Player 1: A / D move · W jump · S crouch · J punch · K kick · U sweep · Space / L special · Shift block.

Player 2 (local): ← / → move · ↑ jump · ↓ crouch · Numpad 1 punch · Numpad 2 kick · Numpad 3 sweep · Numpad 0 / number 0 special · Right Shift block. Number-row 1 / 2 / 3 also work.

Press I for your signature when ready, or a standard grab while it recharges. Hold Down and press I for a standard grab at any time. Player 2 uses 4. Press Enter to skip an entrance or victory scene.

Chain up to three punches and kicks. Try K K J, J K J, J K K, or J J J. Tap the next attack while the current one is playing.

Hold block to reduce damage. Jump over sweeps. Crouch beneath standing punches. Build DOMINANCE by landing attacks. A full bar or health at 25 or below unlocks your fighter special. Emergency use is once per round. Practice starts with a full special bar and unlimited time. R restarts a match. Esc pauses.
